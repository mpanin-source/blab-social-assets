Happy Way already makes every flavour feel like its own little world.

The packaging is doing more than swapping a colour. Each pouch has its own cast, texture, mood and appetite cue.

I wanted to see what the campaign photography would look like if it followed the same rule.

Then I pushed the creator frames one step further: make each drink feel like a dessert moment while keeping it unmistakably a protein shake. Open pouch, scoop, blender—and the little ritual before the first sip.

Cookies & Cream gets the cold blue, milk and cookie crumble.

Double Choc Chip gets the violet, cocoa and dark chocolate.

Caramel Biscuit gets the amber, speculoos and caramel.

Same visual system. Three different flavour worlds. One range that still feels unmistakably Happy Way.

Caramel Biscuit is probably the one I would stop on first. That warm biscuit palette just works.
